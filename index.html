About · 03

About me

Electronics engineer with a focus on embedded systems and IoT.

I'm an electronics engineer with a background in embedded systems and IoT. I completed my B.Tech in Electronics Engineering at Shri Ramdeobaba College of Engineering and Management in 2025, and I'm now doing my MS in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Northeastern University in Boston.

During my undergrad I worked across research and industry. At MaprGo I built ESP32 and STM32 systems that streamed GPS and sensor data to the cloud over MQTT. In the lab, I designed a solar-powered wearable that tracks UV exposure without external charging, and presented it at CIS 2025.

+30% energy efficiency and +25% measurement accuracy, both delivered in firmware.

I also hold patents in hardware-focused product innovation and work in Python (OOP), SQL and networking. This fall I'm a Teaching Assistant for Embedded Design Enabling Robotics, and I'm looking for co-op, internship and full-time roles in embedded systems, IoT and hardware.
